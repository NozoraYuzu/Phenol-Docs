Credit is welcome for any use. However, providing credit does not mean that Nozora Yuzu guarantees the quality, legality, or other aspects of a post or work, and credit must not be presented as an authoritative endorsement.

These Guidelines are governed by Japanese law. In the event of any discrepancy in interpretation, the Japanese version prevails.
- These Guidelines do not exclude the application of mandatory laws in any country or rights that cannot legally be excluded.
- If any provision is held invalid, the remaining provisions continue in effect to the extent permitted by law.

### Contact

As a general rule, Nozora Yuzu does not respond to requests for individual permission checks, advance reviews, or legal advice. No individual contact is required for uses that fall within the conditions of these Guidelines.

## Definitions
- Freely Available: Made publicly available by Nozora Yuzu without requiring a purchase or paid subscription. This does not include unauthorized uploads, works available only to paying members, purchase bonuses, or similar materials.
- Covered Works: The works specified in Article 1 below.
- Individual: A natural person who has reached the age of majority in their place of residence. Because the Covered Works are intended for adults, minors are not permitted to share them or create fan creations based on them.
- Fan Creator: A person who personally creates a fan creation and holds legitimate rights in that creation.

## Article 1. Covered Works and Scope
Works published by Nozora Yuzu and whose rights are also managed by Nozora Yuzu are Covered Works under these Guidelines.
- If Nozora Yuzu specifies separate terms of use for a particular work, those terms also apply.
- For works that require permission from someone other than Nozora Yuzu, such as jointly created works, please follow the separately specified conditions or obtain permission from all necessary rights holders.

The permissions granted under these Guidelines are non-exclusive and do not constitute a waiver or transfer of Nozora Yuzu’s copyright.
- You do not need to seek individual confirmation from Nozora Yuzu for lawful uses such as quotation or private use.

## Article 2. Use by Legal Entities
Legal entities must contact Nozora Yuzu in advance before using works or creating fan creations, regardless of whether the activity is commercial or non-commercial, its scale, or its purpose. Such use requires express, individual permission from Nozora Yuzu.
- This includes not only for-profit companies but also incorporated associations, foundations, nonprofit corporations, educational corporations, religious corporations, foreign legal entities, and similar entities.
- Use carried out as part of a legal entity’s operations falls under this Article even if the actual creator or person posting the material is an Individual. The same applies where a legal entity plans or funds a project sold under an Individual’s name, or acquires rights in a fan creation from an Individual for business use.

The absence of a reply to an inquiry or of an express indication that negotiations will begin does not constitute permission. This Article does not promise a response to an inquiry or the granting of permission.

## Article 3. Sharing and Reposting Freely Available Works by Individuals
Individuals may share and repost works that are Freely Available on social media, personal websites, and similar platforms.
- For security reasons, reposting executable files, such as game programs and applications, is not permitted unless otherwise stated. Instead, please share the location where Nozora Yuzu distributes them.
- The works must likewise be accessible free of charge at the destination where they are posted.
    - Advertising is not restricted, but please be mindful, from an ethical standpoint, of so-called dark patterns.
- Credit is appreciated but not required.
- Changes to file size or format are permitted to the extent necessary for posting.
    - Editing that distorts the meaning of a work is not permitted.
    - Removing signatures or rights notices, or falsely claiming authorship, is also not permitted.
- Uses involving creative alterations, such as translation, musical arrangement, or game creation, are governed by Article 4.

The permission granted under Article 3 does not, of course, include selling products whose primary value lies in reproductions of the works. Nor may mere reposts or copies with only minor alterations be sold under the label of fan creations.

Sharing or reposting through media primarily focused on politics or religion is outside the permission granted under Article 3. This applies even if the particular post is unrelated to politics or religion.

## Article 4. Fan Creations by Individuals
Individuals may create, publish, and distribute fan creations based on the Covered Works, including illustrations, novels, comics, musical arrangements, videos, games, merchandise, three-dimensional works, and cosplay works.
- Except for use for political or religious purposes, no blanket restrictions are imposed on the content of expression or the media used for publication. However, please implement appropriate content labels and audience restrictions in accordance with the laws and cultural norms of the place of publication.
    - This does not mean that Nozora Yuzu takes a position of promoting or enforcing such content segregation. It is included as a necessary practical measure to avoid disputes.
- Here too, credit is appreciated but not required.

In addition, Individuals may create translations of works that are Freely Available and publish those translations free of charge.
- You may not require payment to access the translations where they are published.
- Credit is mandatory for translations. Please clearly identify Nozora Yuzu by name, social media account, or similar attribution.

Materials with little creative input, such as redistributed data or copies with only minor alterations, are governed by Article 3.
- The retention of expression from the original work in translations, musical arrangements, and similar creations is not prohibited solely on the basis of this provision.

## Article 5. Common Conditions
- You may not impersonate Nozora Yuzu or characters from the Covered Works.
- Uses that infringe third-party copyrights, trademark rights, privacy, or other rights, and unlawful uses, are not permitted.
- Nozora Yuzu does not supervise or guarantee users’ activities and does not assume responsibility for transactions or disputes with third parties arising from those activities. This does not disclaim any liability that cannot legally be excluded.

### Section P. Politics and Religion
The Covered Works and fan creations based on them may not be used in any activities related to politics or religion.
- Such activities include, but are not limited to, expressing opinions, making appeals, election campaigning, proselytizing, and promoting organizations.
- “Primarily focused on politics or religion” in Article 3 refers to media whose profiles or posted content indicate that communicating about politics or religion is a principal purpose.

Whether sharing Covered Works or publishing fan creations, you may not make representations that misleadingly suggest that Nozora Yuzu or characters from the Covered Works support a particular position or organization.

### Section Q. Rights of Fan Creators
Copyright arising in creative expression newly added to a fan creation belongs to the Fan Creator or other legitimate rights holder.
- The rights of Nozora Yuzu and other rights holders in the original work are reserved.

These Guidelines do not transfer copyright in fan creations to Nozora Yuzu. Use by Nozora Yuzu is subject to the limited scope of Article 6 or a separate agreement.
- Nor do these Guidelines grant third parties the right to freely reuse expression original to a Fan Creator.

## Article 6. Nozora Yuzu’s Creative Freedom
Article 6 establishes limitations on claims concerning similarity to the Covered Works and a limited license for unintentionally overlapping expression. It is not intended to transfer all rights in fan creations to Nozora Yuzu.

### Section R. Nozora Yuzu’s Creative Activities
Regardless of the existence or publication date of any fan creation, Nozora Yuzu may independently create, publish, and use sequels to the Covered Works, separate series, new works, settings, characters, plot developments, designs, and other elements.
- Elements that are identical or similar to those in fan creations may arise. Nozora Yuzu has no obligation to comprehensively review fan creations or compare them against future official works.

### Section S. Limitations on Demands Based on Fan Creations
Fan Creators agree not to demand that Nozora Yuzu cease creating, publishing, or selling Covered Works, modify or destroy them, pay money, provide credit, or take similar action on grounds such as a fan creation’s earlier creation or publication, or its identity or similarity to the Covered Works.
- However, this Section does not prevent infringement reports supported by specific grounds or the legitimate exercise of rights. It does not alter any burden of proof established by law.

### Section T. Unintentionally Overlapping Expression
Fan Creators grant Nozora Yuzu a worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive license, for the duration of the relevant copyright, to use additional creative expression that they have authority to license, but only to the extent that all of the following conditions are met:
- In the course of creating a work, Nozora Yuzu has included identical or similar expression without intentionally adopting specific creative expression from the fan creation.
- The use is limited to what is necessary for the publication of the official work containing that overlapping expression.
- The use does not fall within the exclusions in Article 6, Section U.

Within the above scope, this license includes reproduction, translation, adaptation, modification, dramatic performance, musical performance, screening, public transmission, exhibition, distribution, transfer, lending, and use of adapted works. Fan Creators agree not to demand license fees or cessation of publication, or make other copyright-based claims, in relation to uses within this licensed scope.

Article 6, Section T is intended to provide a limited safeguard for situations in which reliance on a prior work may become a legal issue. It is not limited to cases in which wholly independent creation can be proven, nor does it constitute an admission that Nozora Yuzu has viewed, relied on, or infringed a fan creation.

### Section U. Exclusions
The following are not covered by the license in Article 6, Section T:

- Direct reposting, distribution, sale, translation, or adaptation of a fan creation.
- Knowingly incorporating into an official work specific creative expression original to a fan creation.
- Exemption from liability for infringement caused by Nozora Yuzu’s intentional misconduct or gross negligence, or for conduct constituting an abuse of rights.

If Nozora Yuzu wishes to undertake any such use for which permission is legally required, a separate agreement with the rights holder is necessary. This Section does not recognize any new exclusive right merely because unprotected ideas or commonplace expressions are shared.

### Section V. Moral Rights
Solely within the scope of the license in Article 6, Section T, Fan Creators consent to modifications ordinarily necessary to incorporate the expression into official works and to uses that do not identify the Fan Creator by name, and agree not to exercise moral rights within that scope.

This is not a request for a blanket undertaking not to exercise moral rights in connection with Nozora Yuzu falsely claiming authorship of a fan creation, uses that harm an author’s honor or reputation, the transfer of moral rights, or similar matters.

### Section W. Commencement and Survival
Article 6 does not apply retroactively to fan creations that existed before its establishment.

Licenses and consents that have been validly established are not revoked solely because the fan creation is no longer publicly available, the user ceases their activities, or these Guidelines are revised or cease to be publicly available. They remain in effect within their specified scope and duration.

- This does not prejudice any rights that cannot be excluded, including legally available rights of termination or rescission based on a breach by Nozora Yuzu.

### Section X. Authority to License and Transfer of Rights
If a fan creation has co-creators, Article 6 of these Guidelines must be explained to all of them, and the necessary rights and consents must be obtained from each rights holder. You cannot license third-party rights that you do not hold.

If you transfer or license rights in a fan creation to another party, you must disclose the licenses and consents under these Guidelines to that party. Do not dispose of or register rights in a manner that conflicts with those licenses and consents.

## Article 7. Unsolicited Ideas
As a general rule, Nozora Yuzu does not accept proposals for settings, characters, projects, or similar material intended for official adoption. Nozora Yuzu has no obligation to review, retain, return, or evaluate submitted materials, or to respond as to whether they will be adopted.

Neither an unsolicited submission nor Nozora Yuzu’s voluntary viewing or introduction of a proposal means that, at that point, a decision to adopt it officially, the start of a collaboration, or an agreement on payment, attribution, or confidentiality has been established. Nor does it mean that Nozora Yuzu becomes free to use the expression in that proposal. Official adoption requires a separate express agreement.

## Article 8. Inquiries and Responses
Requests for permission from legal entities, specific reports of rights infringement, and notices of serious defects in these Guidelines may be submitted through the contact method given in the Preamble. The explanations in the Preamble do not exclude communications concerning individual rights issues.

Nozora Yuzu’s viewing of an inquiry, or failure to respond or take action, does not constitute permission, a guarantee of legality, or a waiver of rights.

An individual response or permission is limited to the relevant party, work, purpose, and conditions. It does not constitute permission for other uses or similar cases, or an amendment to these Guidelines.

## Article 9. Responses to Violations
Nozora Yuzu may request correction or cessation of uses that violate these Guidelines. Where correction is possible, a reasonable period to respond will be allowed as appropriate to the circumstances. Immediate cessation may be requested in cases of serious violations or urgency.

The legal consequences of a breach of these conditions depend on the applicable law and the specific circumstances. A minor omission in attribution or other required notices alone is not intended to render every past use unauthorized.

## Article 10. Revision and Termination
Any revision or termination will be announced in these Guidelines or through an official notice. The treatment of licenses and agreements already established depends on their terms and the applicable law. Unfavorable changes will not automatically apply retroactively merely because they have been announced.

For works whose publication or distribution began before a revision, continued publication and distribution of the same version are permitted as long as the previously applicable conditions are observed. New works and revised versions released after the revision are subject to the revised conditions.
