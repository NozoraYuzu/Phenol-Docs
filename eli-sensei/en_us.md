# Description of the bot program for Teach Us, Eli-Sensei!
Last updated: November 11, 2026

⚠️Currently, Eli-sensei is in beta. It may be unstable.

## 1. Personal Data
This program (hereinafter, Eli-sensei) collects the following information in the channels `#eli-sensei` and `#eli-deletion`:
- Your Discord user ID and username
- Messages you send to Eli-sensei
- The date and time you interacted with Eli-sensei and its content

The collected data is used to provide Eli-sensei's features and improve its quality. Personally identifiable data will not be disclosed to third parties (other than the service provider and yourself).

Also, Eli-sensei only monitors the channels `#eli-sensei` and `#eli-deletion`. Eli-sensei does not accept commands in any other channels.

## 2. Data Deletion
You can unregister anytime with `/unregister`. After sending the command, you can choose one of two deletion methods.
1. Delete everything … Deletes your account info and all your questions Eli-sensei is holding.
2. Account only … Deletes your account info, but keeps your questions, with the poster info (Discord ID and username) anonymized.

Either way, your data is kept for 7 days after you unregister, and then permanently deleted.

If you use `/register` again within 7 days, everything is restored to how it was. The deletion method choice is reset too.

### ⚠️About questions that have already been adopted
The fan site itself is managed by a separate system, not Eli-sensei.

An adopted question has already been handed off elsewhere. Unregistering cannot retract or delete it. Please keep this in mind before agreeing.

## 3. Acorns and Questions
With Eli-sensei, you can register your questions by spending the in-app currency called "Acorns".

For the purposes of this service, the one-month period extending from the 5th of the current month to the 4th of the following month (Japan Standard Time) is referred to as a "tally period," and the period spanning from midnight (00:00) on a given day until just before midnight on the following day is referred to as a "day".

### Acorns
- If you are subscribed to "Institute Patron" or "Blessing of Beginning", you can use `/check-in` to receive 20 Acorns once per day. This action can be performed up to 15 times per tally period.
- If you are subscribed to "Blessing of Beginning", you will automatically receive 200 Acorns at the start of each new tally period.
- To clarify that Acorns do not constitute "prepaid payment instruments" under Article 3 of the Act on Settlement of Funds, they expire after six tally periods (six months). Acorns cannot be purchased directly, exchanged for cash, transferred, or have their expiration dates extended.
- You can check your current Acorn balance and expiration status using `/my-profile`.

### Questions
- You can register one question for the current tally period via `/question` by spending 200 Acorns. You may register up to two questions per tally period.
- If you cancel a registration via `/question` within the same tally period, the 200 Acorns will be refunded. Once the tally period changes, it is no longer possible to cancel a question (except by deleting your account).

## 4. Others
Eli-sensei may change or discontinue the service without prior notice. Additionally, there is no guarantee that a response will be provided for submitted questions.
